# Programme library sources and adaptation policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

The Banner Fitness library contains PT-owned starting points, not automatically approved client programmes. Each template must be reviewed against the client record, screening information, equipment, schedule, experience, preferences, recovery and goals before it is saved or assigned.

## Established frameworks reviewed

- [ACSM resistance-training position stand and infographic](https://acsm.org/education-resources/pronouncements-scientific-communications/position-stands/) — evidence context for flexible assessment of strength, hypertrophy, power, frequency, effort and volume. This informs the quality engine; it is not a single programme prescription.
- [StrongLifts 5×5 workout guide](https://stronglifts.com/stronglifts-5x5/workout-program/) — reference for the recognisable full-body 5×5 novice-strength structure and alternating sessions.
- [Jim Wendler books and programmes](https://www.jimwendler.com/collections/books-programs) — reference for organising main-lift strength waves. Banner Fitness uses its own PT-owned template and does not reproduce the commercial programme or instructions.
- [Barbell Medicine Beginner Prescription](https://www.barbellmedicine.com/the-beginner-prescription/) — reference for adapting beginner options around schedule, equipment and starting experience. The source includes commercially supplied material, so Banner Fitness uses only high-level structure and its own exercise prescriptions.
- [Barbell Medicine The Bridge](https://www.barbellmedicine.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/FREE-Bridge-Template.pdf) — reference for the transition from novice linear training to an intermediate-style strength structure. The Banner Fitness template is an original adaptation, not a copy of the PDF.
- [GZCLP overview](https://liftvault.com/programs/powerlifting/gzclp-program-spreadsheets/) — reference for tiered main, supplemental and accessory organisation. The page is a secondary source; it is used only to understand the public framework, not to reproduce its spreadsheet.
- [Stronger by Science programme bundle](https://www.strongerbyscience.com/program-bundle/) — reference for novice and hypertrophy template categories and block-based thinking. No proprietary template text or prescription is copied.

## What was added

The catalogue grew from 10 to 30 templates. The 20 additions cover:

- novice and intermediate strength frameworks;
- 2-, 3-, 4- and 6-day structures;
- machine/dumbbell, barbell-minimalist, bodyweight, travel-band, suspension and rings options;
- hypertrophy volume, push/pull/legs and emphasis templates;
- concurrent strength and conditioning;
- running support and field-sport power support.

## Safeguards

Named programmes are treated as inspiration or framework references, not as permission to copy a paid product. A library template is never a clinical or medical prescription, never bypasses screening or referral, and never replaces the qualified PT’s decision. The client-specific editor and dynamic quality review remain mandatory before approval.
